## Enable GitHub Pages

In the repository, open **Settings → Pages** and configure:

- Source: Deploy from a branch
- Branch: `main`
- Folder: `/ (root)`

Save and wait for GitHub Pages to publish the site. The URLs above will then become available.

## Important before publishing

These are starter legal templates, not legal advice. Review and complete every item marked **[ACTION REQUIRED]** before submitting the app to Google Play:

1. Confirm the legal operator's exact name and contact email.
2. Confirm the app's actual data practices, permissions, SDKs, and AdMob configuration against the current build and Google Play Data Safety answers.
3. Review the applicable AdMob / Google Play disclosures and consent requirements for the countries in which the app is distributed.
4. Have the documents reviewed for the operator's jurisdiction and intended markets.

**Do not describe the app as collecting no data** simply because core gameplay runs offline. Advertising SDKs may collect or process data independently of gameplay storage.
